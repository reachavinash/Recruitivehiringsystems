About us

Structured hiring. Scalable growth.

Recruitive Hiring Systems is a recruitment agency based in Sector 58, Noida. We exist because most hiring fails for boring, fixable reasons: unclear briefs, unscreened profiles, slow feedback and candidates who never hear back.

We run a deliberately structured process instead — define the role properly, source with intent, screen before submitting, and keep both sides informed at every stage. It is not glamorous, but it fills roles faster and with fewer surprises.

What we stand for

Four principles we do not bend on

Honesty over volume

We only submit candidates we would genuinely recommend. If a profile is not right, we say so.

Precision over speed alone

Fast matters, but a fast wrong hire is expensive. We screen against a written scorecard.

Respect for candidates

Every applicant gets an update. No ghosting, no vague timelines, no surprise offers.

Transparency in commercials

Fees, replacement terms and payment schedules agreed in writing before we start sourcing.

Our process

From brief to signed offer

The same four steps run for a single role or a bulk drive — only the volume changes.

  1. Intake & role definition

    We document must-haves, nice-to-haves, compensation band, location and interview panel availability.

  2. Deliberate sourcing

    Talent pool, referrals and targeted search. No mass application dumping.

  3. Screening & shortlist

    Telephonic screening, motivation check, notice period and CTC alignment — submitted with fit notes.

  4. Interview to offer

    Scheduling, feedback collection, negotiation support and follow-through until joining.
